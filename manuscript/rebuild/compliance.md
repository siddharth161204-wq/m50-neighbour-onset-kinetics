## Not met, and open

1. **The Fire Safety Journal's page preference.** Its guide prefers original research papers of no more than 20 double-spaced pages including tables and illustrations; the rebuilt manuscript is 33. Reaching 20 would mean cutting about two-fifths of the paper, for example three of the seven figures, the two appendices and about a third of the main text, and so dropping one of the six results sections. The guide says "preferably", and the manuscript meets the other two journals' limits as it stands; if the Fire Safety Journal is chosen, the main chat decides whether to cut or to submit at this length.
2. **The Journal of Energy Storage and the supplement.** That journal asks for "detailed procedures necessary to replicate the study, as well as additional results such as sensitivity analyses" in an appendix, and keeps the supplement for media. If it is chosen, Supplementary Sections S5 (the checks), S6 (the bound inputs) and S7 (the digitisation) would move into an Appendix C as they are, adding two tables; the figures and tables together would then be 12, above that journal's ideal of 8 to 10.
3. **The author's own entries.** The postal address of the affiliation (an independent researcher's own address, with the country); the generative AI statement under its heading; the competing-interest form in the submission system; and the choice among the five titles in `titles.md`.
