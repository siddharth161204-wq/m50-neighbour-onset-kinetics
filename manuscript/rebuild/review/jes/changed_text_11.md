## The abstract (page 1), all of it

Propagation models must assume the heat a failing cell (the trigger) gives its neighbour and whether each cell is one temperature (lumped) or a temperature field (resolved). Sixteen tests of touching LG M50 pairs are modelled with NCM811/graphite kinetics, the resolved neighbour in three dimensions. Fitted to peak temperatures, trigger energies are 31.6 to 54.3 kJ in fourteen (strong triggers) and 9.0 and 16.6 kJ in two (weak). Most exceed calorimetry's largest, 33.2 kJ, perhaps because the trigger's flame heats the thermocouple the energy is matched to. With the trigger's energy fitted or held to 33.2 kJ, conduction and radiation run no resolved neighbour away. A need is the least heat that, delivered to the neighbour's facing side (the half facing the trigger), runs it away by the measured time; a threshold, the least that does so at all. Over 21.4 s, the mean rise time derived from two published records, the eight propagating strong-trigger tests' needs are 4.45 to 7.54 kJ resolved, two of them thresholds, and 0.26 to 5.96 kJ lumped. At nominal inputs, the resolved threshold is at least 1.36 times the lumped one after four strong triggers searched in three dimensions, at most 0.91 times after both weak ones. A neighbour's share of vented heat calibrated in a lumped model may need correcting for a resolved one, in a direction that goes with the trigger's energy. Published inputs rule out no path delivering 27.9 kJ within 21.4 s; a facing-side heat flux could test the needs.

## Introduction, second paragraph (page 2): its first three sentences, of which the citations of the first and the wording of the second and third changed

Pack models represent each cell as one temperature [feng2015propagation] or resolve it [mishra2021multimode,hoelle2023simulation], and let a neighbour run away when its temperature reaches an onset value or when Arrhenius heat sources outrun its losses. Their heat paths are conduction, radiation and convection [mishra2021multimode]. Models of one cell represent what it vents, as a share of its energy carried off with the ejecta [coman2017modelling] or as the vent gas and its reactions [kim2021venting].
