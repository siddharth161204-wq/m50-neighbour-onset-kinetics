# Candidate titles

Three titles of at most 20 words each, the preferred first (Brief 20, M1). The main chat chooses.

1. Neither conduction nor a fixed delivered energy fraction reproduces sixteen two-cell propagation tests: a pre-registered kinetic study (17 words)
2. Local and whole-cell ignition criteria for thermal runaway propagation, tested against sixteen two-cell LG M50 experiments (16 words)
3. How much ejected energy a conduction model needs to reproduce two-cell thermal runaway propagation, and why no fixed fraction works (20 words)

Why the first is preferred: it states the two negative results the paper rests on, both of which hold in the files (no run with the trigger's body energy ignites the neighbour; the propagated and non-propagated tests overlap in the fraction they need, so no single fraction reproduces the sixteen outcomes), and it names the pre-registration, which is what distinguishes the study. The second is the most neutral, and fits a journal that prefers descriptive titles. The third leads with the inverse reading, the paper's most reusable number, but at the word limit.

"Conduction" in the first title means conduction and radiation from the trigger's body; with each test's own, larger trigger, conduction alone does ignite the hot spot in eight tests, minutes early (Section 3.3), so the title's claim is that conduction does not reproduce the tests, not that it never ignites.
