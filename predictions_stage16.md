## The quantities

- T_L, the level: 255.68 C. C8: the first time within 900 s at which the resolved neighbour's mean temperature (weighted by heat capacity) reaches T_L. C2: the same for the lumped neighbour.
- Every run is Stage 15's (`stage15_run.run_one`, unchanged): one test of Fransson et al. with its fitted trigger, the central contact (2,000 W/(m2 K)) and tabs, 25 C, the posterior-mean kinetics, the fresh SEI, the nominal properties, the base mesh (19,355 nodes on the half cell) and step limits unless stated, both neighbours from the same trigger history, an energy E delivered through the facing half from t = 0, the resolved neighbour stopped at C8 or at 900 s and the lumped twin run to 900 s.
- The deliveries: the 21.39 s triangular pulse; constant power over 120, 240 or 360 s. E_e = 3.105 E_b is a test's ejected energy, used only for the searches' grids.
- t_m: the test's measured time (Table S7). A run is early if its C8 comes no later than t_m + 10 s, and late if its C8 comes later or not at all within 900 s.
- The need, by Section 3.2's definition: the least delivered energy with which C8 comes within 10 s of t_m, counted as delivered by the runaway. Its bracket is (the highest late energy below every early one, the lowest early energy], counted as delivered by t_m + 10 s, where the need's runaway comes: with the pulse, which ends at 21.39 s, the whole energy; with constant power, the energy times the share delivered by then. Where the runaway at the threshold already comes more than 10 s before t_m, the bracket straddles the threshold, its lower end without C8 (the case marked t in Table S7).
- E_x: the reactive cross-section's need for the same test and delivery (Table S7; `results/stage15_table.csv`, C8_xs_E_J, and by its runaway C8_xs_E_by_t_J). d = (E_x - m) / m, with m the midpoint of the three-dimensional need's final bracket, both counted as delivered by the runaway.
- The lumped need: Table S7's lumped need for the same test and delivery (C2_E_by_t_J): with the pulse 4,851.0 J (PT-Exp4, at its threshold), 2,974.4 J (PP-Exp1), 5,962.4 J (PP-Exp2), 1,819.0 J (PP-Exp4), 1,184.0 J (ST-Exp4), 2,725.6 J (SP-Exp1), 3,783.4 J (SP-Exp2) and 263.5 J (SP-Exp3).
- PT-Exp1's thresholds with the pulse: the least energy at which C8 happens within 900 s (in three dimensions, by T below) and at which C2 does (the lumped: (12,848.8, 12,892.4] J, the narrowest archived bracket).

## The searches, the registered list

- N. For each of the eight tests that propagated after strong triggers (PT-Exp4, PP-Exp1, PP-Exp2, PP-Exp4, ST-Exp4, SP-Exp1, SP-Exp2, SP-Exp3), with the pulse. Every three-dimensional run already made in the same configuration counts: the archived runs that Stage 15 set the cross-section against and Stage 15's B1 runs. The next run: with no early run, the lowest point above every late run of the grid E_x + k g (k = 0, 1, 2, ...); with no late run, the highest point of the grid below every early run (k = -1, -2, ...); with both, the bracket's midpoint; until the bracket is at most 0.00125 E_e wide (155 to 211 J in these tests). g = 0.0025 E_e, and at most eight grid steps are taken either way (out of range beyond). A search in which a late run lies above an early one ends as not monotone.
- T. PT-Exp1's threshold with the pulse by Stage 15's B1 rule: runs at the cross-section's E*(C8), 10,781.25 J, less and plus 5 % (10,242.2 and 11,320.3 J); then, while both reach C8 or neither does, a step of 10 % of that value on the side needed, until a run with C8 lies one step above one without. The bracket is (the highest without C8, the lowest with C8].
- V. Once no N search is running: among the needs found whose bracket's lower end also runs away within 900 s (a need above its threshold), the one with the latest measured time, the nearest above its threshold, where the runaway time is the most sensitive to the model's details (if none, the latest among all the needs found; ties in N's order). Both ends of its bracket are made again with the mesh refined by 1.5 (82,309 nodes) and every step limit halved, together. Where an end changes class, one further refined run one bracket width beyond it on that side, at most two such runs.
- X. As computing allows, once N is done: N's rule with constant power over 240 s, then 120 s, then 360 s, test by test in N's order, until every such search is done or no X run is started because the X runs completed have taken six hours of a worker's time; a search unfinished then is reported as unfinished.
- A run that fails is made again at once; one that fails twice stops its search, which is reported as stopped.

The first runs of every search (each later run is read from the records by the rules above): `s16_T_PT_Exp1_s-1`, `s16_T_PT_Exp1_s+1`; `s16_N_PT_Exp4_tri21_f0.056871102` (7,062.5 J, the midpoint of the archived bracket (6,709.4, 7,415.6]), `s16_N_PP_Exp1_tri21_f0.038048483`, `s16_N_PP_Exp2_tri21_f0.060135489`, `s16_N_PP_Exp4_tri21_f0.041565713`, `s16_N_ST_Exp4_tri21_f0.036345294`, `s16_N_SP_Exp1_tri21_f0.041032714`, `s16_N_SP_Exp2_tri21_f0.042919039`, `s16_N_SP_Exp3_tri21_f0.027170408` (each at E_x but PT-Exp4's); and the 24 first runs of X, listed by `python3 src/stage16_run.py list`.

## Predictions

Each prediction is scored by its rule alone; a search that ends without a final bracket (stopped, not monotone, out of range or unfinished) counts against every prediction that needs its bracket, except where a rule says otherwise. The expectations are from the records named, with their reasons; the estimates of the three-dimensional needs are read from the cross-section's own runs (`results/stage16_expectations.json`), and are estimates.

P1. With the pulse, the cross-section's need lies within 10 % of the three-dimensional need in each of the eight tests. Scored: |d| <= 0.10 in each of the eight; holds if all eight are. Expectation: holds, with d between +2 and +9 %. Reasons: (i) the cross-section's need is the energy of a run whose runaway came within 10 s of t_m on either side, and its search stopped there or at its resolution of 0.005 E_e; by its own runs, read linearly in 1/t, its tabulated need lies 1.0 to 8.3 % above its own least energy with a runaway by t_m + 10 s (most in ST-Exp4, 8.3 %, PP-Exp4, 5.5 %, and PT-Exp4, 4.5 %, whose tabulated needs are at the resolution of their thresholds); (ii) near the thresholds after strong triggers the three-dimensional runaway came earlier than the cross-section's at the same energy, by 10.5 s (PT-Exp4 at 7.42 kJ), 15.5 s (PP-Exp2 at 7.20 kJ) and 7.9 s (SP-Exp3 at 4.06 kJ), read the same way. With a lead of 10 to 20 s, the cross-section's runs put the three-dimensional needs at 7.09 to 7.11 kJ (PT-Exp4), 5.64 to 5.78 (PP-Exp1), 7.37 to 7.46 (PP-Exp2), 5.67 to 5.70 (PP-Exp4), 5.07 to 5.08 (ST-Exp4), 5.80 to 5.85 (SP-Exp1), 6.05 to 6.18 (SP-Exp2) and 4.31 to 4.40 kJ (SP-Exp3), d = +1.9 to +8.7 %. Against this, SP-Exp3's three-dimensional threshold lay 5 to 15 % above the cross-section's, so in the tests whose needs lie close to their thresholds (PT-Exp4, PP-Exp4 and ST-Exp4, t_m of 272 to 342 s) the three-dimensional need could lie nearer E_x or above it. In PT-Exp4 the archived bracket (6,709.4, 7,415.6] bounds d between +0.3 and +10.9 %.

P2. With the pulse, the cross-section's need lies above the three-dimensional need in each of the eight tests. Scored: E_x > m in each of the eight; holds if all eight. Expectation: holds, for the two reasons of P1; fixed in PT-Exp4 by the archived bracket's upper end (7,415.6 J, below its E_x of 7,440.2 J); least sure in PP-Exp4 and ST-Exp4, for the reason P1 gives.

P3. The range survives: with the pulse, every three-dimensional need's bracket lies within 4.1 to 8.1 kJ. Scored: in each of the eight, the bracket's lower end is at least 4,050 J and its upper end at most 8,150 J (4.1 and 8.1 kJ to the paper's rounding); holds if all eight. Expectation: holds; the estimates of P1 lie between 4.31 and 7.46 kJ, and the archived runs already put SP-Exp3's need above 4,056.9 J and PP-Exp2's above 7,196.9 J, and PT-Exp4's within (6,709.4, 7,415.6].

P4. With the pulse, the resolved need exceeds the lumped need in each of the eight tests. Scored: in each of the eight, the three-dimensional bracket's lower end is at or above the lumped need (above, to Table S7's 0.1 J); holds if all eight. Expectation: holds, and is fixed, or nearly, by earlier records in seven of the eight: a three-dimensional run already made at the lumped need's own energy (PP-Exp1, PP-Exp4, SP-Exp1, SP-Exp2) or above it (PT-Exp4 at 6,709.4 J, PP-Exp2 at 7,196.9 J, SP-Exp3 at 4,056.9 J) is late, so the bracket's lower end cannot lie below it unless the search ends not monotone. In ST-Exp4, with no three-dimensional run made, the lumped need is 1.18 kJ against the cross-section's 5.51 kJ.

P5. With the pulse, the resolved need exceeds the lumped need by 1.0 to 5.0 kJ in each of the eight tests. Scored: in each of the eight, the bracket's lower end lies at least 1,000 J above the lumped need and its upper end at most 5,000 J above it; holds if all eight. Expectation: holds, by 1.4 kJ (PP-Exp2) to 4.1 kJ (SP-Exp3) on the estimates of P1 (the cross-section's differences are 1.7 to 4.3 kJ); PP-Exp2's lower end is already 1.23 kJ above its lumped need.

P6. After PT-Exp1's weak trigger the order reverses in three dimensions: the resolved threshold with the pulse lies below the lumped threshold. Scored: T's final bracket's upper end is at most 12,848.8 J, the lumped bracket's lower end; holds if so. Expectation: holds. The cross-section's threshold, 10,781.25 J, is 0.84 times the lumped threshold; in three dimensions the threshold lay within 5 % of the cross-section's in three of the four tests searched (PT-Exp4, PP-Exp2 and ST-Exp3, the last after a weak trigger, 16.6 kJ, where the three-dimensional runaway came 20.5 s after the cross-section's at 10.80 kJ) and 5 to 15 % above it in SP-Exp3; within 15 % above, T's bracket ends at most at 12,398.4 J.

P7. PT-Exp1's ratio of the resolved to the lumped threshold, by the brackets' midpoints, lies between 0.75 and 0.95. Scored: R = m_T / 12,870.6 J, m_T the midpoint of T's final bracket; holds if 0.75 <= R <= 0.95. Expectation: holds, at 0.84 (0.79 to 0.89 across the brackets, rounded outward) if the first two runs close the bracket, in which case its midpoint is the cross-section's value by construction and the three-dimensional runs say only that the threshold lies within 5 % of it; 0.92 if it lies 5 to 15 % above, 0.75 if 5 to 15 % below. ST-Exp3, the other weak trigger, gave 0.85 (0.80 to 0.91).

P8. The refinement moves V's need by at most one bracket width. Scored: with the refined runs alone, the refined bracket is closed and lies within (lo - w, hi + w], (lo, hi] being the base bracket and w its width: both ends keep their class, or one end changes class and the extra refined run beyond it does not; it fails if both ends change class, if the extra run also changes class, or if a refined run fails twice. The change is reported in joules and in per cent of the need, beside the change in each end's C8. Expectation: holds, most likely with both ends keeping their class. In Stage 15's V4 the mesh refined by 1.5 moved the runaway of PT-Exp4's 7,415.6 J run, at the upper end of its threshold's bracket, from 181.92 to 184.62 s (+1.48 %), and the halved step limits to 181.94 s; at the 6,709.4 J lower end neither ran away. A runaway later by about 1.5 %, 2 to 5 s at t_m + 10 s of 157 to 352 s, changes an end's class only where its C8 lies that near t_m + 10 s, while the bracket's two ends, at most 0.00125 E_e apart, differ in C8 by about 10 to 60 s near the needs, by the cross-section's slopes of 0.07 to 0.3 s per joule.

P9. The refinement moves the runaway time by less than 3 % at both ends of V's bracket. Scored: for each end whose base and refined runs both reach C8 within 900 s, |C8 refined - C8 base| / C8 base < 0.03; holds if every such end is within (an end with C8 on one mesh only counts as not within). Expectation: holds, at about +1.5 % (V4's +1.48 % and +0.01 %, above).

P10. The cross-section's needs with constant power lie within 10 % of the three-dimensional needs found by X. Scored: |d| <= 0.10 for every X search closed, both counted as delivered by the runaway; holds if every one is; not scored if X closes none. Expectation: fails in at least one search. With constant power the cross-section's runaway came 1.3 to 34.5 s after the three-dimensional model's in seven of the eight archived pairs of these tests in which both ran away, and 0.6 s before it in one (PP-Exp1 over 240 s at 3.97 kJ, 34.5 s; PT-Exp4 over 240 s at 6.60 kJ, 30.1 s; PP-Exp1 over 360 s at 5.95 kJ, 23.1 s; PT-Exp4 over 360 s at 8.93 kJ, 10.0 s; PP-Exp2 over 240 s, 9.8 s, and over 360 s, 7.8 s; SP-Exp2 over 240 s, 1.3 s, and over 360 s, -0.6 s), and with constant power the runaway time changes slowly with the energy, so that by the cross-section's runs a lead of 10 s gives d = +13.9 % in PP-Exp1 over 240 s and +10.4 % in SP-Exp2; in PT-Exp4 over 240 s the archived runs bracket the three-dimensional need within (6,209.2, 6,597.3] J, so its d lies between +2.7 and +9.1 %.

P11. With constant power, the resolved need exceeds the lumped need in every X search closed. Scored: for every X search closed, the bracket's lower end counted as delivered by t_m + 10 s is at or above the lumped need for the same test and delivery (Table S7, to its 0.1 J); holds if every one is; not scored if X closes none. Expectation: holds; the cross-section's needs with constant power exceed the lumped ones by 2.8 to 4.0 kJ, and in 21 of the 24 cases a three-dimensional run already made at the lumped need's energy or above it is late (none is made in PP-Exp4 over 240 s or in ST-Exp4 over 240 or 360 s).
