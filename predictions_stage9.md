# Stage 9 predictions: the delivered fraction read from each test's time and peak

Written on 29 October 2026, before any Stage 9 run. This file, Part A0 (DECISIONS.md D89, pushed before this file was written), the Stage 9 decisions (D90 to D97), the code that will make the runs (`src/stage9_run.py`, `src/stage9_model.py`, `src/stage9_search.py`, `src/stage9_inputs.py`, `src/stage9_laptop.py`, `run_stage9_windows.cmd`), the scoring script (`src/stage9_score.py`) and the checks (`src/stage9_checks.py`) are committed and pushed to the public repository before any Stage 9 run, and the commit hash and push time are recorded in the README and in `provenance/`. The eight predictions are the brief's, word for word, each with the brief's expectation; on the author's answers of 29 October (D90 (2)), P6 is scored on M50_PT_Exp4, M50_PP_Exp2 and M50_ST_Exp3, and P8 on M50_PP_Exp4, M50_SP_Exp1 and M50_SP_Exp2, in place of the brief's "three median tests", two of which did not propagate. The rules below fix what the brief leaves open.

When this was written, no run of the neighbour in a Stage 9 configuration had been made. The code was tested on synthetic inputs only, and three sizing runs with cell 1 held at the ambient temperature (no trigger) were made, two of them stopped once their cost was clear (D95 (6)). But Stage 7's and Stage 8's records already bear on most of these predictions, and the next section says how, prediction by prediction, so that a verdict that was foreseeable is read as one.

## Definitions used for scoring

- **f\*** (D78): Stage 8's Part A bracket (lo, hi] or 0; under a long delivery or the aged SEI, D78's search replayed along its own path (D93 (2)).
- **t_c**: the time the criterion is first met, C1 the hot spot and C2 the lumped neighbour at 255.7 C, interpolated within the step; none if it is not met within 900 s. The model's clock runs from the start of the trigger's release, the paper's from the trigger's runaway (D34, D89 (3)).
- **f_t** (D91 (3)): the fraction of the bisection's run whose crossing lies within 10 s of the measured time (matched), or the fraction interpolated linearly in t_c between the final bracket's two ends where both cross (bracketed); not found otherwise (threshold, early, above grid, not monotone). Found means matched or bracketed.
- **f_max** (D92): 0 where the thermocouple's peak at f = 0 is at least the measured maximum or within 5 K below it; otherwise the fraction of the run whose peak lies within 5 K of it, or the fraction interpolated linearly in the peak between the final bracket's ends.
- **Below** (D92 (5)): f_max lies below f* when f* is not 0 and f*_lo is at least f_max's upper end.
- **Within 30 s**: the absolute difference between a run's t_c and the measured time is at most 30 s.

### How each is scored

- **P1.** The nine propagated tests, the 21.39 s delivery. Holds if no C1 run of any search, Stage 7's at f = 0 and Stage 8's included, at a fraction at or above f*_lo(C1), crosses within 30 s of the measured time. The latest crossing of each test is reported.
- **P2.** The nine propagated tests, the 21.39 s delivery. A test counts if some C2 run at a fraction f with f*_lo(C2) < f ≤ f*_hi(C2) + 0.1 crosses within 30 s of its measured time. Holds if at least six count. The run nearest in time is reported for each.
- **P3.** The tests P2 counts. For each, f_t(C2) of A2 where found, else the fraction of P2's run nearest in time; holds if every one less f*_hi(C2) is at most 0.02. Each difference is reported.
- **P4.** The five registered tests. Holds if, in every one, f_max lies below f*(C1) and below f*(C2) by the rule above (ten relations). Each relation is reported.
- **P5.** The five registered f_max and the f_t(C2) found among the nine. Holds if the largest f_max is smaller than the smallest f_t(C2), by their values; the margin, the two brackets and whether the brackets alone separate them are reported, with the count of f_t(C2) found.
- **P6.** M50_PT_Exp4, PP_Exp2 and ST_Exp3, constant power over 240 s. Holds if every one has an f_t(C1) found and every one exceeds 0.2. A test without one is reported with its search's end (for instance "threshold" or "above grid").
- **P7.** Holds if the two factors r_c / r, rounded to two decimals, are 0.75 and 1.40 and P5, recomputed with every f_t and f_max times each factor, gives the same verdict.
- **P8.** M50_PP_Exp4, SP_Exp1 and SP_Exp2, the 21.39 s delivery. Holds if, in every one, both f_t(C2) (aged and fresh) are found and differ by less than 0.01. Each change is reported.
