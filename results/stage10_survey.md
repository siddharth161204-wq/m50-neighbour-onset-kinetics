# Stage 10, Part D: the survey of other datasets

Recorded on 30 October 2026 under DECISIONS.md D106 (1), after the predictions were pushed. The survey records what exists; it runs nothing, reads no parameter from a figure and adopts no other cell's properties. Its machine-readable record, with every search and every page read, is `results/stage10_survey.json`.

## The question

The brief asks for every published dataset found with per-test outcomes and times for cell-to-cell propagation in cells in direct contact or at a stated spacing (at least Hoelle, Zimmermann and Hinrichsen 2023; Lamb et al. 2015; Said, Lee, Stoliarov and Marshall 2019; and any multi-cell set of the Battery Failure Databank), with the cell, the chemistry, the number of tests, the trigger, what is reported, the licence or access, and whether it could be run with this study's parameters without inventing any, which the brief defines as an LG M50, or a 21700 NMC cell with published thermal and kinetic parameters. This study's parameters are those of the LG INR21700-M50: the thermal properties of O'Regan et al. (2022) and the jelly roll Chen et al. (2021) measured, the B-CRNN kinetics of an NCM811 cathode and a graphite anode (D1), the reactant masses of the kinetics paper's 25 Ah NCM cell (D4, D7), the trigger's energy from the Databank's M50 rows, and the two-cell study's contact conductances.

## Where the survey looked

The web search tool was queried 26 times on 30 October 2026 (the queries are in the JSON), in English and once in Chinese. Every candidate was then read on its publisher's or repository's page where that page could be fetched: the full texts of Read, Jones and Marco (2024), of Read, Mathew and co-authors (2024), of Sun and co-authors (2025) and of Allen and co-authors (2025) from the University of Warwick's repository, the Sandia report behind Lamb et al. (2015) from OSTI, the Battery Failure Databank's page and its 2024 paper, the NLR mirror of Fransson et al. (2024), Lee's thesis from the University of Maryland, and the records of the other papers. The Project's own files were searched too; they hold the Databank's paper and a thesis on single-cell data, and no multi-cell set. Four hosts could not be read: IOP Science refused the fetch tool (its robots rules), so the Electrochemical Society papers were read from their abstracts and repository records; ResearchGate returned an error; mediaTUM's bot protection refused the request; and the NREL documents host refused it, so Fransson et al. (2024) was read from its NLR mirror. Where a detail below rests on an abstract or a companion source rather than the article itself, the entry says so.

## The one that qualifies

One dataset meets the brief's test: Read, Jones and Marco (2024). Its cell is the LG INR21700-M50, its seven cells sit at a stated spacing of 3 mm, and each of its six tests reports the outcome and, where the test propagated, each neighbour's time. It is marked here as the qualifying dataset, and the survey stops. What a run on it would need beyond this study's parameters is recorded for the decision in the main chat, not taken here.

- The heat path. Neighbours do not touch: the 3 mm gap's conduction and radiation follow from air's published properties and the study's emissivity, but the four FR4 sheets that hold the cans touch them, and that contact is not reported, so it would enter as a bracket.
- The trigger. The nail enters the centre cell's positive terminal axially, about 10 or 35 mm deep, where Fransson et al. nail the side; the trigger cell's maximum surface temperature is reported for every test (315.1 to 595.1 C in the three shallow tests, 1051.5 to 1248.1 C in the three deep ones), from which the trigger's energy would be read as in Stage 7 and a delivered fraction as in Stage 9.
- The mechanism. Every test whose trigger cell ruptured at the side propagated to all six neighbours within 2 to 5 s; none without rupture propagated, and its neighbours peaked at 49.7 to 60.9 C. A conduction model has no term for the ejecta of a sidewall rupture, so of this dataset only the three tests without rupture, and the split by rupture, lie within what it represents.
- The data. The paper's tables hold the outcomes, times and maxima; its histories are in figures, and its raw data are available on request.

## P8

P8's first clause holds: datasets other than this study's report per-test outcomes and times for cells in direct contact (Fransson et al.'s P42A tests, Lamb et al.'s pouch modules, Said et al.'s arrays with no gap, and Schöberl et al.'s stacks). Its second clause fails: one dataset, Read, Jones and Marco (2024), has a cell that this study's parameters cover without an invented value, the LG M50. P8 therefore fails. The setting of that dataset differs from this study's in the heat path, the trigger and the mechanism, as listed above.
