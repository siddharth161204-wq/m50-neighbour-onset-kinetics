# Stage 13: sentences with their numbers

These are proposals for the main chat. They are not placed in `manuscript/`, which this stage does not open. Each number is in `results/stage13_paper_numbers.json`, `results/stage13_scorecard.json` or the Stage 13 tables, and DECISIONS.md D139 to D143 say where it comes from.

## For the manuscript's revision

**The mechanism of each clock.** The local threshold is conduction into the can and the outer layers of the roll. A conduction model of the mid-height cross-section with no reaction lies within 3 % of every new resolved threshold for triangular deliveries from 5 to 120 s (Fourier numbers 0.025 to 0.60) and inside all sixteen archived brackets at 21.39 s. With the reaction switched off at the upper end of each new search up to 120 s, the hot spot still reaches 255.7 C or falls short of it by at most 1.1 K. The whole-cell threshold is an energy balance to an ignition temperature: the temperature at which the cell's reaction heat, three quarters of it from the first cathode reaction, overtakes the lumped neighbour's loss, 218.5 C with the trigger at the neighbour's own temperature. A two-body model that also asks whether enough of the first cathode reaction's 2,667 J is left to reach 255.7 C reproduces the lumped thresholds within the larger of 10 % and 300 J in all 32 new searches at the low and the high contact.

**The conditioning.** The whole-cell threshold is a difference. It is the heat needed to bring the neighbour to its ignition point, less what the trigger conducts, so the needed delivery falls by about 0.4 J for every joule of the trigger's body energy (0.37 to 0.44 J per J in thirteen tests). One step of 0.0125 in the archived f*(C2) brackets therefore corresponds to 3.3 to 4.8 kJ of a trigger's body energy, to 32 % or more of the contact conductance, or to a shift of 18 to 20 K in the temperature at which the reactions run. In that sense the brackets constrain the model's inputs only loosely. The 73- to 109-fold spread of f*(C2) across the tests is the shape of that difference near its zero. From the triggers' energies alone, the two-body model gives a 475-fold spread at 21.39 s, set by the one test it places 150 J from zero.

## For Professor Deng (the posterior as a distribution of ignition temperature)

Read through the lumped neighbour's heat balance, the 200 posterior draws of the decomposition kinetics become a distribution of ignition temperature. Its central 95 % runs from 215.3 to 221.4 C, a span of 6.1 K, with the trigger at the neighbour's own temperature. The first cathode reaction gives most of the reaction heat at that point in every draw (57 to 85 %).

## For Professor Ghoniem (the regimes)

The two thresholds belong to two regimes separated by the Fourier number of the delivery, alpha tau / a^2, with alpha the roll's thermal diffusivity across its layers (5.565e-7 m2/s), tau the delivery's duration and a the cell's radius (10.55 mm). For the 10 s and 21.39 s pulses (0.05 and 0.11), the heated skin is under a third of the radius: the local threshold is set by conduction into it and lies 2.9 times below the whole-cell one at 21.39 s in the runs, and 4.2 times below it at 10 s in the reduced models. By a 360 s delivery (1.8) the cross-section is heated through and the local crossing is a runaway of the reaction on the cell's axis, 18.9 K beyond what conduction alone reaches at the surface.

## For Professor Battiato (the map in the Fourier number)

Against the Fourier number alpha tau / a^2 (alpha the roll's thermal diffusivity across its layers, tau the delivery's duration, a the cell's radius), the ratio of PT_Exp1's whole-cell to local threshold falls from 2.90 at 0.11 to 1.16 at 0.60 and to 0.94 at 1.80 in the runs (at 0.11 the whole-cell value is the archive's refined bracket, the others are new). The two reduced models' ratio traces the same curve from 5.9 at 0.025 to 0.87 at 1.8, so a single map in the Fourier number places the manuscript's deliveries: the 10 and 21.39 s pulses in the skin regime, the 60 s pulse and the 120 s power between, and the 240 and 360 s powers in the through-heated regime (the map itself was traced with triangular deliveries).

## For Professor Sapsis (the reduced models as the cheap fidelity of the module study)

Each threshold that costs a resolved search of two to seven runs, of 49 to 620 s each, is given in about a minute by the cross-section model, within 3 % up to a Fourier number of 0.6. The whole-cell threshold comes from the two-body model in about a minute, within the larger of 10 % and 300 J of the lumped model in all 32 new searches at the low and the high contact (and in 29 of the 32 with the triangular 120 and 360 s deliveries). This makes the pair a cheap fidelity level for a multi-fidelity study of modules, with its limit marked: the cross-section model overstates the local threshold by 12 % once the delivery is long enough for the reaction to run away.
