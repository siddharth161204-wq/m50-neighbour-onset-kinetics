# Stage 15: sentences for the main chat

Proposals, with their numbers, for the main chat to use, change or drop. Stage 15 is on branch `stage15`, not released. Every number is in `results/stage15_paper_numbers.json`, `stage15_scorecard.json`, `stage15_table.csv` or `stage15_cross_section.json`. Throughout, the resolved neighbour runs away (C8) when its mean temperature, the heat-capacity-weighted mean over the cell (its stored heat over its heat capacity), reaches 255.7 C within 900 s; the lumped neighbour is the same cell as one temperature; both carry the posterior-mean kinetics of Koenig, Zhao and Deng and are heated by the same archived trigger and the same delivered energy.

## For the manuscript's revision

1. With the same kinetics, the resolved neighbour itself runs away only when about 7.1 kJ is delivered over 21.4 s in M50_PT_Exp4 (between 6.7 and 7.4 kJ) and 6.9 kJ in M50_PP_Exp2, 1.49 and 1.72 times the energies at which the lumped neighbour does, and 3.9 kJ in M50_SP_Exp3, whose lumped neighbour runs away with no delivery at all; only with M50_ST_Exp3's weak trigger does the resolved cell need less than the lumped one (10.3 against 12.0 kJ).

2. The whole-cell clock's agreement with the measured times therefore belongs to the lumped cell: for the resolved cell itself to run away at a test's measured time, a reactive cross-section that predicts its thresholds finds that it must have had 4.1 to 8.1 kJ delivered by then in every reproduced case but M50_ST_Exp3, whatever the delivery's duration, where the lumped cell needs 0.2 to 6.0 kJ; and in M50_PT_Exp4 the resolved cell's far face reads 234 C when its own jump begins, 37 K above the 197 C from which the measured neighbour jumped.
